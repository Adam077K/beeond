## Intended artistic direction

> The artistic direction I want is grounded in real-world details: people, objects, places, landscapes, and color. These real and human visuals should be combined with an additional layer — either subtle or dramatic — of ASCII, pixels, typography, or other effects.
>
> The goal is to create a distinctive balance between **life and the real world** and **technology**.
