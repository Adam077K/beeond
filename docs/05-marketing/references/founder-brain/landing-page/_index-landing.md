## PART 2 — Full-page teardowns

Attention key: **LOUD** = demands the eye, big scale / high contrast / imagery.
**MEDIUM** = holds attention when reached. **QUIET** = deliberately recessive.

**Text density:** thin everywhere. The only real prose on the page is inside customer
quotes and inside the fake chat transcript. Nothing else exceeds three lines.

**How it explains an intangible:** four devices, in this order — (1) a *transcript*: the

than reads about it; (2) *four hard numbers* immediately after the logo wall; (3) a
*named three-part model* (Platform / Practice / Experts) that gives the service an
architecture; (4) *repeated per-customer metrics* — every case row leads with one number
(42%, 84%, 943% ROI) before the quote.

**Worth stealing:** the hero's proof object is a transcript of the work happening, not a
picture of a tool.
