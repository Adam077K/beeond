## What these two add

1. **A product capability can be shown as a loose card stack.** Neither file shows an app screenshot. Both show *artefacts* — a logo mid-edit, a type specimen, an asset on a transparency checkerboard — arranged with deliberate overlap. Directly relevant to a company with a dashboard it cannot yet screenshot.
2. **Depth is created by overlap and soft shadow, not by glass.** Both stack cards at varied z-depth on one flat or gently-graded ground. Only one element in either file is actually glass.
3. **Real nature photography sits inside a brand system without being the hero** — magnolia, mountains, sky, all as small cards among type specimens and diagrams.
