### 1. `B04-blurred-cloud-code-text-openai-codex-graphic.jpg`
**Founder:** "A clean, minimal white website. Color is mostly introduced through images and cards in the hero section. Additional layered elements create a premium, high-quality, and polished feeling."
**What's in the frame:** A 736×411 landscape key-art graphic — not a website, not a screenshot, no cards and no white. A flat sky-blue field fills the whole frame; across the middle drift soft, heavily blurred iridescent cloud forms in magenta, lavender, peach and pale yellow, like an out-of-focus oil slick. Scattered across them are small white monospace code fragments (`shape.as_li`, `dynamic = tf`, `def s`, `tmax`), broken mid-token and set at low opacity so they read as texture rather than as legible code. Two wordmarks sit large, centred on one shared baseline in a plain white bold geometric sans: **Codex** on the left, **OpenAI** on the right.
**Match check:** `MISMATCH — this is an OpenAI Codex promotional graphic on a sky-blue ground. There is no white website, no hero section and no cards in this file.` The likeliest explanation is that the founder was describing the *page* this asset was lifted from (the OpenAI/Codex site is white and minimal, and this graphic is its hero image) while the file captured only the hero asset. That is an inference, not something visible here — **treat the note as being about a page we do not have, and this file as evidence only for the layered-hero-graphic half of it.**
**Operative for us:** Colour and depth come from one layered image element; the surface behind it stays plain.

### 2. `C04-dithered-clouds-blue-halftone.jpg`
**Founder:** "I like the feeling of ASCII or another overlay placed over part of an image. It creates a good, slightly dreamy atmosphere, with a strong color combination."
**What's in the frame:** A small, near-square (355×327) photograph of cumulus clouds against sky, reduced to a coarse two-tone halftone dither. Only two inks are present: a saturated royal/cobalt blue for the sky and white for the clouds, with mid-greys produced purely by dot density — the shadowed cloud mass at lower-left is almost solid dot-black. The dot grid is large enough to be seen as dots at 100%, so the image reads as printed matter rather than as a photo. No type, no logo, no framing device.
**Match check:** `PARTIAL — the dither is applied uniformly across the entire frame, not "over part of an image". There is no separate overlay layer and no ASCII; the whole photo has been converted.`
**Operative for us:** Dither a photograph into two visible-dot inks; let the dot grid stay coarse enough to see.

## What repeats across these eight

1. **Sky is the background, not a subject.** Six of eight sit on sky or a flat sky-blue field (B04, C04, B15, B09, B21, C21) — sometimes photographed, sometimes reduced to one flat ink.
2. **A dot or halftone screen is what makes an image feel authored.** C04 and B15 are built from visible dots; B04 uses scattered monospace fragments the same way. The texture is always coarse enough to see.
3. **One human gesture, mostly empty frame, small type.** Hands (B09, B15), one face cropped past its edges (C06), one person against sky (B21, C21). Type — where present — is small, knocked out in white or cream, sitting directly on the image with no box or scrim (B04, C06, B10).
