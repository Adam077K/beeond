## 3. Three strongest, and why

**A-01, the hero on a hand-made frame.** It beats the current proposal on the law itself. A swarm *near* a hand is proximity; a swarm confined to the rectangle two hands make is a mask taken from the subject, and it cannot be lifted onto another photograph. It also says the thesis in the right order: the human sets the boundary, the swarm fills it.

**A-30, the anatomy-keyed swarm.** It turns the hero's motion from drift into obedience. Density propagating along the hand's own direction makes the field belong to the person, which is the difference between the site's argument and a screensaver.

**A-14, the colour-mode conflict.** Moment 1 is defined as conversion; §5's hero is overlay. The budget currently believes a moment is spent that is not. Cheap to settle now, expensive after a renderer exists.

**A-32 sits just behind them,** because it rescues a flagged-weak assignment with a physical fact rather than a filter.

## 4. What the index gets wrong about what is in the frame

Three genuine errors, one omission, and one note about a document adjacent to the index.

**A08 — the cloud is not filled with text.** The index says "the cloud's shape is filled entirely with repeated small typeset text". In the frame the type is *not confined to the cloud*: the cloud sets the ragged top edge of a text block that then runs on across open blue sky as a full-width justified mass for most of the poster's height. The cloud itself is largely clean white.
