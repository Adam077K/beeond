## Patterns across the ten (findings, not advice)

- **Nav carries the ask: 9 of 10.** SAGE is the only exception, and it is also the only site whose primary ask appears once.
- **The ask is a conversation, not a purchase: 10 of 10.** Only Designjoy also offers direct checkout, and it still offers a 15-minute call beside it.
- **The ask repeats three or more times on the homepage: 9 of 10.** Four placements is the mode: nav, hero, mid, close.
- **One label reused everywhere: 5 of 10** (Kalungi, Powered by Search, Inter-Dev, Fletch, Mersel). Omnius uses four different labels for the same ask.
- **Client-identity proof sits in section two: 8 of 10.** Designjoy defers it to section five, SAGE to section three.
- **Client logos appear: 10 of 10. Testimonials: 9 of 10. Case studies on the homepage: 7 of 10. Hard numbers: 8 of 10.** No site of the ten carries zero client-identity proof.
- **A step-by-step process is shown: 7 of 10.** The three without are the three traditional agencies (SAGE, Inter-Dev, Omnius).
- **FAQ on the homepage itself: 5 of 10**, plus 2 more linking one from the footer. Every FAQ found handles cost, timeline and fit.
- **Comparison or versus table on the homepage: 2 of 10** (GrowthSpree, Mersel). Both are the AI-native entrants arguing against an incumbent category.
- **A number for price on the homepage: 4 of 10** (Designjoy, Mersel, Fletch, NoGood). Two more state a pricing shape with no rate.
- **A guarantee: 3 of 10** (Powered by Search, Designjoy, Mersel).
- **Explicit disqualification of a buyer: 2 of 10** state a hard refusal (NoGood, Omnius); 2 more gate by company stage or ARR (Kalungi, Fletch).
- **Named humans as proof: 6 of 10.**
- **A services or solutions item in the nav: 8 of 10.** The two without are the two single-offer productized businesses, whose nav is offer, proof and price instead.
- **A dedicated proof destination in the nav: 8 of 10**, variously Case Studies, Results, Portfolio, Wall of Love, Before/After.
- **A dedicated CTA block immediately before the footer: 6 of 10.** Four close on content instead: blog, resources, downloads, testimonials.
- **An ask reachable above the fold: 10 of 10**, and in the hero body itself in 9 of 10.
- **The three sites with the thinnest client proof carry the most structural risk-reversal.** Designjoy, Mersel and Fletch hold 3 of the 3 guarantees found, 3 of the 4 published prices, and 3 of the 5 homepage FAQs.
