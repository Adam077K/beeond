## A. SITE ARCHITECTURE

Four pages, replacing every prior name — `POSITIONING.md`, `MESSAGING.md`, `GTM_STRATEGY.md`, `CHANNELS.md`, `SEO_STRATEGY.md` all carry REOPENED banners and are provenance only.

| # | Page | Slug | Who it's for | What it must accomplish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home** | `/` | Everyone arriving cold — no assumed channel, since traffic source/ICP is undecided | Make the invisible offer legible without a screenshot, establish the swarm+human+dashboard mechanism, and move a qualified stranger to Book a Call. Carries the full narrative arc. |
| 2 | **Approach** | `/approach` | A visitor who believed Home's premise and wants the mechanism proven out — how does "swarm executes, human calibrates, dashboard proves" actually run month to month | Depth on the operating model: the three-part architecture, the phased onboarding shape, and what "done" means (the DoD method). This is where the product gets explained with structure instead of a feature grid. |
| 3 | **About** | `/about` | A visitor doing a trust check before booking — who are the two people actually doing this work, and how new is this company really | Put Adam and Yarden on the page by name, and state the company's actual stage (zero clients, zero revenue) as a trust device rather than hide it. This is the page that does the buyer-trust research's job: named founders, founder-led content, radical transparency about being new. |
| 4 | **Book a Call** | `/book-a-call` | A visitor who is ready to talk, or ready to leave contact info if not | Minimum-friction conversion: the call ask dominates the page. Waitlist is the fallback for anyone not ready today. |

**Why these four and not four others:** every alternative that would normally round out a services site — Pricing, Case Studies, a Blog — requires evidence Beeond does not have (pricing is undecided per `HANDOFF-CLEAN-START/04-THE-PRODUCT.md` §10; case studies require delivered client work, and there is none; a blog requires a content pipeline that doesn't exist). Approach and About are the two pages that build trust and explain the mechanism using only what is actually true today — the founders, the operating model, and the honest stage of the company — without inventing anything.

**What was considered and rejected:** a `/pricing` page (blocked — no tier or dollar figure survives the reset as more than a method); a `/work` or `/case-studies` page (blocked — zero delivered engagements, would require fabricated proof); a `/blog` (no content pipeline exists yet, and it competes with Approach for the "how we think" job at a fraction of the craft).

### Nav

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Logo (home link) | Mark, left |
| Nav links, in order | Approach · About |
| Primary CTA | "Book a Call" — accent-filled button, right of nav, persistent across all four pages |

## E. WHERE A SIGNATURE MOMENT WANTS TO LAND

Not an assignment — the later dispatch owns that. Noted by natural pull only, and each of the seven is used exactly once across the *whole site*, not per page.

- **Home §1 (Hero)** — strongest pull of any section on the site. Dramatic end of the tech dial is explicitly where Layer 2 says to push hardest. Knockout logotype (moment 7) or the ASCII/glyph field (moment 1) both fit a dramatic opening statement.
- **Home §2 (The architecture)** — the blueprint/construction overlay (moment 6, "the page shows its own working") is a near-perfect conceptual fit for a transparency company explaining its own mechanism. Worth strong consideration even though it competes with the hero for attention.
- **Home §3 (Proof / capability display)** — honeycomb mesh screen (moment 5) has thematic pull given the hive-cell mark geometry, and would sit naturally behind or around the artefact cards.
- **Approach §2 or §3** — if the blueprint overlay isn't spent on Home, the phased-onboarding section is its second-strongest candidate: dimension lines and baseline rules over a process diagram reads as exactly what it is.
- **The pinned scroll-scrubbed set-piece** (Layer 4b, separate from the seven signature moments) — strongest natural placement is **Home §4 or §5** (process or what's-covered): both sit below the fold, neither is a LOUD section that would double up the attention budget, and a swarm-converging or hands-and-data motion reads naturally against either.
- **Home §9 (Close)** — a quieter candidate: motion blur / long exposure (moment 3) as a warm, hopeful closing note is the gentler alternative to spending something dramatic here twice.

## F. WHAT YOU LEFT OPEN

1. **Actual headline/body copy.** This spine names the job, belief, and device per section — it does not draft the typed content layer itself. That is the next step under `WEBSITE-DESIGN-PROCESS.md` G2 scope, distinct from this architecture pass.
2. **Booking widget implementation.** `WEBSITE-DESIGN-PROCESS.md` §9 item 3 logs lead-capture plumbing (booking + waitlist) as deferred, with no backend. Book a Call §2 is designed to *dominate* the page today on the assumption a real widget lands there later; what it is until then (embed, form, mailto) is unresolved.
3. **The "why now" / moat framing on About §4.** `HANDOFF-CLEAN-START/01-THE-IDEA.md` §5's honest moat assessment is an internal document written for the founders, not buyer-facing copy — every claimed moat source has a documented counter-argument. How bluntly to carry that honesty onto a page a prospect actually reads is a copy-and-calibration call for Yarden, not a structural one.
4. **FAQ content on Home §8 and Book a Call §4.** The objections addressed are structurally inferred (price uncertainty, "why so new," "will this look generic") — not sourced from a real buyer conversation, since zero customer interviews have happened. Flagged as a content gap, not filled with invented quotes.
5. **Traffic–message match.** Cannot be audited yet — no channel or traffic source is decided (ICP is open), so there is no inbound message to match against. Left open until a channel exists.
6. **Nav labels.** "Approach" vs "How it works," "Book a Call" vs "Talk to us" — copy-level naming, deferred to the actual copy pass rather than decided here.
7. **Signature-moment assignment.** Explicitly out of scope for this dispatch — §E above is pull, not assignment.
8. **Whether Home's 9-section length is right.** The reference set's full pages run 10–14 sections (Ada 11, Agentwork 10, Jasper 10, Superside 14, Speakeasy 12); this spine's 9 is on the leaner end, closer to Agentwork's restraint. A leaner cut (folding "what's covered" into "the architecture," for instance) is a legitimate alternative not explored further here.
