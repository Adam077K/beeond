## 2. Per-image notes

### C01-dithered-dot-portrait-art-poster.jpg
**Frame.** Woman's eye and nose in warm rust close-up; event title built from white square dots; mono details
run vertically up the right margin.
**Technique.** Ordered dither building *type* rather than image. The letterforms are dense at the core and
scatter into isolated single dots at their edges — the site's own edge physics applied to a glyph. The poster
splits at a horizontal tonal seam and the lower block of dot-type is mirrored. Headline is illegible as
type and functions as texture; the legible information is the small mono block.
**Ideas** → C-01, C-02.
**Note.** The rule this implies: dot-built type must be either fully legible or explicitly texture. Half-way
is where it reads as a bug.

### C02-ascii-dot-horse-overlay-photo.jpg
**Frame.** Two horses running in snow, faded scratched print; a cream dot-and-plus field draws a **third**
horse, offset right, mid-stride, overlapping both.
**Technique.** Ghost twin, overlay mode, monochrome marks, opaque, photo at full strength through the gaps.
Density is heavy along the ghost's leading contours and thins to single marks at the trailing legs and tail.
The base photograph is heavily degraded, which is the only reason the crisp marks read.
**Ideas** → C-03, C-04, C-05.
**Correction.** See §5.1 — this is not a traced silhouette.

### C03-perplexity-brand-collage-desert-sand.jpg
**Frame.** Three panels: illustrated desert seen through a window; a reclining person rotated into the right
frame edge under blossom hanging down from the top; giant wordmark over sand.
**Technique.** Panel 2 puts wordmark, mark and tagline on **one horizontal baseline across open sky** while
the human is cropped hard by the right edge and gravity is contradicted by frame rotation. Panel 3 sets the
wordmark solid white, overrunning both frame edges, over raw macro texture.
**Ideas** → C-06, C-07.
**Correction.** See §5.2 — rotated, not inverted, and the distinction matters because inverted photography
is on the "not used" list.

### C04-dithered-clouds-blue-halftone.jpg
**Frame.** Cumulus against cobalt sky, whole frame converted to two-colour ordered dither. No type, no logo,
no untouched area.
**Technique.** Uniform Bayer dither, total conversion, occupancy modulation. No subject relationship beyond
tone, no anchor.
**No idea, and why.** `ART-DIRECTION-BRIEF` §2 certifies this as the one reference in the founder's set that
**fails the law**, and every plausible home for it — a sky ground, a texture band, a loading tile — is
exactly the uniform conversion that constitutes the failure. Recording it as a negative is worth more than
forcing a use.
**Worth carrying forward anyway:** dither is *occupancy* modulation and H5's halftone is *radius*
modulation. They are different mechanisms under one moment name, so if the founder ever wants both, the
budget question is real and should be answered explicitly rather than by argument about naming.

### C05-ascii-butterfly-over-forest-photo.jpg
**Frame.** Cream ASCII moth, wings spread, over a blurred grainy dark-green forest; its thorax sits on the
tree trunk, antennae follow the branch forks.
**Technique.** Silhouette-clipped glyph field, monochrome overlay, character ramp. Density is near-solid at
the upper wings and almost all gaps at the lower ones, so the creature reads as materialising downward. The
creature's anatomy is keyed to the photograph's own structure — the strongest instance of the law in the
whole slice.
**Ideas** → C-08, C-09.

## 5. What the index gets wrong about what is in the frame

Six, ordered by how much they change a downstream decision.

**5.1 C02 — "tracing the silhouette of the horses on top."** It is not a trace. The dot field draws a
**third** horse, offset to the right of both real animals and in a different stride phase, overlapping them.
`ART-DIRECTION-BRIEF` §2 already says "becomes a third animal" and §3 names the mechanism *ghost twin*, so
the index also contradicts the brief. This is load-bearing: trace and ghost twin are two of the four
combination methods, and the hero depends on which one the bee uses.

**5.2 C03 — "an inverted-photo hero."** The photograph is **rotated**, not inverted. A reclining person
enters from the right frame edge and blossom hangs down from the top; gravity is contradicted by frame
rotation, not by a vertical flip. Load-bearing because *inverted photography* is on `DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md`'s
"not used" list, so the file is currently catalogued under a technique the site has excluded, when what it
actually does is permitted.

**5.3 C07 — "over a dark mossy-green background."** There is no background. The **whole frame** is
converted to characters, including the dark areas, so there is no un-effected anchor anywhere — which is
the compositing rule this image breaks and the reason it reads as a texture rather than a subject. The
index also calls the form a butterfly silhouette; it is ambiguous in the frame and reads as a moth or a
plant. Separately, the characters run in vertical columns with visible streaking, which gives the field a
direction that conflicts with the site's single diagonal.

**5.4 C10 — "a cream note/index-card layout."** It is a **plot**: a hairline cell grid with three dots
positioned at different cells, each carrying a word label, and a rotated axis label at the card's left
edge. Load-bearing because the plotted-annotation register is what §17.2 depends on, and C10 is its
cleanest instance in this slice.

**5.5 C12 — "a halftone-dot-overlaid team photo on the right."** The halftone is the lesser half. The
photograph's top and left edges **disintegrate into scattered rectangular tiles** carrying photographic
fragments, clustering at the corner and thinning outward. That is the site's locked mosaic dissolve, in the
wild. The invariant currently cites no source, and this is one.

**5.6 C14 — "Section order: Nav → Hero → AI-agents panel → Brand-intelligence panel → Integrations
panel."** It is not a vertical sequence. It is a **bento on one screen**: a tall dark cell spanning two rows
at left, a large cell at top right, two smaller cells beneath. Load-bearing because §9 picks grid species
from the corpus, and this file is the corpus's clearest bento while the index describes it as a stack.

**5.7 C18 — "a glowing blue pixelated/mosaic gradient blob graphic."** Not a blob and not a gradient.
Nested **stepped square apertures**, hard-edged tiles at several scales radiating from a bright core, with
no blur in the frame at all. Load-bearing because "gradient blob" invites the gradient-mesh reading that is
on the tell list, whereas the actual technique is quantised, which is exactly why it is usable.
