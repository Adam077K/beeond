**Angle: THE MECHANISM-FIRST SITE.** Every section below is judged first by whether it makes one of the
three mechanism parts — swarm executes, human calibrates, dashboard proves — legible as a specific,
sequenced, checkable thing, rather than a tagline. Where a section does not clearly serve one of those

Architecture: the default four pages — **Home · Approach · About · The Ask Page.** Kept as-is; no fifth page

Every voice string below is **PROVISIONAL** per §0.7. Markers used throughout: **PROVISIONAL** (voice),
**(A)/(B)** (unresolved alternatives, not picked).
