## 1. What Beeond is

The definition is stable across every founding document. The fullest formulation:

> "**Beeond** is a **bilingual (Hebrew + English) done-for-you marketing & growth agency for B2B / SaaS / tech companies**, that runs a client's **entire digital footprint** as a single coordinated **swarm of AI agents + two expert founders**, delivering agency-quality work at a speed and price no traditional human agency can match."
> — `docs/01-foundation/FOUNDING_BRIEF.md`, §1

The two founders: **Yarden Morgan** (marketing & growth) and **Adam** (AI agents / swarms / systems). No employees. (`FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §1)

Per this session's founder decision, the germ of this definition — the Beeond name, the swarm thesis, and a bilingual HE+EN B2B footprint agency — is what carries forward into the reset. Everything downstream of it (offer, tiers, pricing, service catalog, build sequence, and the ICP itself) is reopened. See §4 below for exactly which of the founding decisions that touches.

## 2. The name and the swarm metaphor

**Beeond = Bee + (bey)ond.**

- **Bee / swarm:** wild bees move as a coordinated swarm toward one goal in the hive. Beeond's agents, agent-swarms, and humans all work together toward one goal: the client's digital brand / footprint.
- **Beyond:** signals the future of how marketing gets done — AI agents that lower price, ship faster, and adapt faster than any agency before.

(`docs/01-foundation/FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §2)

This is concept, not branding, and it carries forward. The visual execution of it — logos, palettes, the "bee/hive" device — does not; that is out of scope for this handoff by founder instruction.

## 3. The four positioning pillars

From `docs/01-foundation/FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §4:

1. **Whole footprint, one swarm** — not a point tool, not a siloed agency. Everything coordinated toward one goal.
2. **AI-native, founder-led** — built and run by people who do AI swarms (Adam) and marketing/growth (Yarden), not an agency bolting ChatGPT onto old workflows.
3. **Speed & price disruption** — automation collapses the cost and turnaround of agency-grade work.
4. **Not generic** — directly answers the #1 SMB fear of AI ("it'll look generic"): human creative judgment and brand-specific brainstorming sit on top of the automation.

## 5. The honest moat assessment

The founding brief is explicit that the moat is **thin today** — "like any new agency." Four planned sources, and the honest counter-argument to each:

| Planned moat source | The claim (`FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §8 / `STARTUP_OVERVIEW.md` §7) | Counter-argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Swarm tech (Adam)** | Proprietary agent-swarm orchestration that compounds — every codified flow lowers cost and raises speed vs. competitors | Compounding is a projection, not an observed effect: zero delivery flows have been codified against a paying client yet. Agent-swarm tooling for marketing delivery is not exclusive to Beeond — the same maturity wave (`VISION.md` "Why Now") that makes this viable for Beeond makes it viable for any well-resourced competitor. |
| **Founder pairing** | Rare combination of real marketing/growth judgment (Yarden) + AI systems depth (Adam) | This is a claim about two people's skills, not a structural barrier. `VISION.md` itself leaves "Execution track record" as `[To fill — founders' prior work / proof points.]` — the pairing has not yet produced a track record that would make it hard to replicate. |
| **Speed & price** | Automation collapses the cost and turnaround of agency-grade work | The brief flags this itself: "a wedge, not a moat alone (copyable) — defensible only when fused with the swarm tech and integrated full-footprint delivery" (`FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §8). Price and speed alone are the easiest thing for a competitor to match. |
| **Integration / data** | Running a client's whole footprint creates switching cost and a cross-channel data advantage over point-solution competitors | This advantage only accrues once a client has actually run multiple channels through Beeond long enough to build history — with zero signed clients, it's theoretical. It also isn't unique to a swarm-delivery model: any full-service agency running a client's whole footprint makes the same structural argument. |

## 6. Decisions later reversed

**The coined-term rejection.** CMO proposed coined umbrella terms for the offer — "Hiveprint" and "Quorum." The founder personally reviewed and reversed toward plain descriptive language instead ("your whole digital presence / entire digital footprint"). Hero copy was locked to a plain, non-coined headline. (`.claude/memory/DECISIONS.md`, entry **2026-06-30 — Hero copy locked: descriptive (no coined term) + speed/AI-era headline**)

**Local SEO / GBP / NAP demoted to optional add-on.** Within the founding interview itself, the offer was scoped so that Google Business Profile, reviews, citations/NAP, and speed-to-lead — services that default to "core" in a typical local-marketing agency package — were demoted to **optional add-ons**, not part of the core B2B/SaaS footprint. (`docs/01-foundation/FOUNDING_BRIEF.md` §3 decision 5, and §6 "RECOMMENDED CUTS / RE-FRAMES for B2B/SaaS")

**The 2026-08-06 English-only call was site-scope, not company-scope.** During a total brand reset the founder set a new market scope. The decision is logged, but **on the unmerged branch `ceo-1-1786028037`, not on `main`** — which is why it is invisible to anyone reading the main line of the repo. Its own wording settles the question:

> "**Market:** international startups, English only. Hebrew / RTL deferred to a later wave; Hebrew is not in scope for **this build**."
> — `.claude/memory/DECISIONS.md` on branch `ceo-1-1786028037`, entry *2026-08-06 — Brand reset: new visual identity locked, Blueprint and all prior visual direction retired*, item 6

"This build" scopes it to the website, not the company. This session's founder decision confirms that reading: **Beeond remains a Hebrew + English bilingual company; the site goes English-first.** Hebrew is also the one piece of white space the competitive research identified (`docs/02-competitive/COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md`, referenced in `STARTUP_OVERVIEW.md` §7: "no current player occupies whole-footprint + AI-swarm + bilingual HE+EN") — dropping it would discard the only named differentiator.

## 7. The name/trademark blocker — an unclosed gate

**Verdict: MEDIUM collision risk.** The name is usable but **not formally cleared.** This has been sitting open since the day it was raised.

> "⚠️ THIS IS A PRELIMINARY COMMON-LAW + WEB SCREEN, NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE. Official trademark registries ... are JavaScript apps or bot-walled and could not be queried directly here. A registered-mark search and likelihood-of-confusion opinion require a trademark attorney. Do not treat 'no exact match found' as 'the name is clear.'"
> — `docs/02-competitive/NAME_CLEARANCE.md`

### Conflicting / confusable marks

| Name | Spelling vs "Beeond" | Field | Class | Jurisdiction | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Beeond Publicidade** | Exact | Advertising / publicity agency | 35 (advertising) | Brazil | Active |
| **Beeond, Inc.** | Exact | IIoT / OPC-UA no-code server software | 42 / 9 | US (New Bern, NC) | Active since 2005 |
| **BeondX** | Phonetic twin ("Beond") | Full-service performance/retention marketing agency | 35 / 42 | India HQ, "100+ brands worldwide" | Active |
| **Beeyond Media** | Phonetic match ("Beeyond") | Digital OOH programmatic advertising | 35 / 42 | Americas + Madrid (NY, Miami offices) | Active |
| **Beyond Agency** | Phonetic/meaning overlap | 360 marketing agency | 35 | — | Active |
| Bee/BeeOn cluster (Bee Marketing, Bee Digital, BeeOn Digital Marketing, BeOnAds) | Partial | Various marketing/advertising | 35 | Various | Active |

(`docs/02-competitive/NAME_CLEARANCE.md`)

**The three that matter most:**
- **Beeond Publicidade** (Brazil) — exact spelling, advertising agency, Class 35.
- **BeondX** — phonetically identical, full-service marketing agency, global.
- **Beeyond Media** — phonetic match, programmatic advertising, NY/Miami.

Trademark examiners and courts assess likelihood of confusion on sight, sound, meaning, and relatedness of services — not exact spelling. "Beeond," "Beond," and "Beeyond" are phonetically identical, and several of these operate in the same Class 35 (advertising/marketing) Beeond would file under.

### What was never closed

- **The gate was set on 2026-06-29 and never completed.** The founder released Design-Lead to build the brand on the condition that "formal trademark clearance (attorney, Class 35+42, US+IL) runs in parallel and MUST complete before any PUBLIC launch." (`.claude/memory/DECISIONS.md`, entry **2026-06-29 — Brand gate released + name-clearance verdict + catalog lock**)
- **Six registries were never queried:** USPTO, EUIPO, Israel ILPO, Brazil INPI, Trademarkia, and Justia all either block automated queries or were simply not reachable in the research pass. The Israel ILPO gap is called out as critical because Israel is a primary target market. (`docs/02-competitive/NAME_CLEARANCE.md`, "Gaps")
- **`beeond.ai` is unsecured.** The domain research recommended registering it immediately as the primary domain (available, on-strategy for an AI-native brand) — no source confirms it was ever purchased. (`docs/02-competitive/DOMAIN_AVAILABILITY.md`)

**Seven website versions were built downstream of a name that was never formally cleared.** The trademark question has been open since the first week of the company's existence and remains open today.
